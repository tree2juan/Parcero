    How to use these

    1. Every card opens in the language you chose to learn. Change that choice at the top of the page and the cards turn around with it.
    2. Answer out loud before you flip it. Pulling it out of memory is what makes it stick — recognising it once you see it does not.
    3. Say you did not know it when you did not. Those cards come back later in the set; the ones you wave through do not.
    4. Short and often beats long and once. A set is meant to be finished in one sitting.
